About Me: My name is Sodaba Sikandary, and I am 15 years old, from Kabul, Afghanistan. I have always had a deep passion for coding and technology. From a young age, I dreamed of becoming a computer scientist and attending Westcliff University to study Computer Science. I was in grade 6 when the Taliban took control of Afghanistan. During that difficult time, I struggled with depression because I loved my school and education, and I didn’t want to see my dreams crushed. However, I never lost hope, and in 2023, I received a scholarship from 21K School. Now, I am proud to be a student here, working towards my future.

My Hobbies: I have a variety of hobbies that keep me inspired and creative. I enjoy drawing, coding, cycling, and reading books. Each of these activities helps me unwind and think outside the box. Coding, in particular, fuels my curiosity and desire to solve problems, which I believe is a key skill for my future career.

My Favorite Countries: There are a few countries that I truly admire. Türkiye, Canada, and Paris have always been places I’ve dreamed of visiting. These countries are rich in culture, history, and diversity, and they inspire me in different ways. I look forward to experiencing their beauty and learning from their unique cultures one day.
